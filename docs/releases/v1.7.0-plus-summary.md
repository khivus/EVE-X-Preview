**EVE-X-Preview v1.7.0.8 — highlights since v1.7.0**

### Added
- Optional standalone modern Settings editor alongside the built-in editor, with shared profiles, validation and additional themes.
- Automatic settings backups, recovery guidance, off-screen thumbnail recovery and separate pause controls for EVE switching or all hotkeys.
- Per-profile incoming-first or outgoing-first DPS display order.

### Changed
- Rebuilt Settings with search, a resizable layout, Apply/Discard drafts and protection against losing unsaved edits.
- Spreadsheet-style assignment tables with copy/paste, keyboard navigation and removal undo; inheritance per section and editing Default in place.
- Redesigned event settings and shared sound library, with faster sound startup.
- Full portable-package updates with rollback and offline recovery; Settings editor downloads install and launch directly.
- DPS and damage-mix text adapt to small thumbnails with compact labels and k/m/b values.
- Clearer shared-shortcut priority and conflict reporting, plus reduced activation delay during rapid client switching (#20, thanks to @S0CT).

### Fixed
- Rapid Settings edits save in order; table selections and dropdown text remain readable across themes.
- More reliable table-cell editing, gesture assignments, profile retention and scrolling through Apply and reconnect.
- Settings focus, text clipping, color editing and painting errors when controls close.
- Foreground thumbnails refresh during held switching, and Drifter battleship pilots are recognized as NPCs.
